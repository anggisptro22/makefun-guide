## How to Choose a Bounty

Discovering a challenge tailored to your expertise is effortless:
1. Explore the **Bounties** directory on the platform dashboard.
2. Filter listings by technology stack, domain category, difficulty level, or bounty reward amount.
3. Review specific requirements, evaluation criteria, and submission deadlines.
4. Pick the bounty that excites you most and lock in your participation.

---

## How to Take the Kit

Every bounty supplies the foundational assets you need:
1. Access the official starter repository or starter kit linked in the bounty details.
2. Clone or download the template framework directly to your local working environment.
3. Read through any accompanying documentation, architecture guides, and configuration references provided in the kit.

---

## How to Build & Submit Your Entry

With your environment initialized, bring your solution to life:
1. **Develop:** Craft robust, clean code adhering to best practices and the precise acceptance criteria specified in the bounty brief.
2. **Validate:** Test your implementation rigorously to confirm functionality, performance, and bug-free execution.
3. **Submit:** Wrap up your work, push your finalized code to your repository, and submit your entry through the make.fun submission portal before the countdown expires.

## How to Claim Rewards

Your dedication deserves instant recognition:
1. Once your submission passes review by judges or automated validation checks, your reward is approved.
2. All payouts on make.fun are settled in **USDC on the Solana blockchain**, guaranteeing lightning-fast transactions, minimal fees, and top-tier security.
3. Link your Solana wallet on your dashboard and hit **Claim** to deposit your USDC earnings straight into your account.
